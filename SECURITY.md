# Security Policy

This repository is public.

Assume everything committed here is permanently visible to the world.

Do not commit:

- passwords;
- API keys;
- tokens;
- secrets;
- private credentials;
- student personally identifiable information;
- private student records;
- private health information;
- private addresses or phone numbers;
- sensitive contracts;
- confidential financial information;
- unpublished high-sensitivity business information;
- private repository contents copied wholesale;
- proprietary material not authorized for publication;
- third-party copyrighted or private content that cannot legally be shared.

If uncertain, do not publish.

Move the material to an authorized private channel or ask the Human.

Public-safe routing notes, metadata, handoff stubs, and reconciliation receipts are acceptable when they do not expose sensitive content.
